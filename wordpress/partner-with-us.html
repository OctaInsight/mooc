Questions

Do learners pay anything?

No. Learners join, learn and receive their certificates for free. Only the organisation publishing the course chooses a package.

Can we use our own certificate design and logos?

Yes. Upload your design as an image or PDF, mark where the learner's name and details go, and every certificate is made on it.

Can we issue certificates for an event without a course?

Yes. Choose "Certificates for an event", upload an Excel list of participants, and each person receives their own certificate by email.

What happens when our project ends?

With the EU project package, your courses and certificates stay online for 3 years after the end, already paid. After that you can continue with Hosting.

Who owns the course content?

You do. You keep the rights to your material; we publish it on the MOOC for as long as your package runs.

How are certificates checked?

Each certificate has a number and its own page at mooc.cloudearthi.com, which shows the learner, the course, your project as issuer, and that it was verified by the CloudEARTHi MOOC and Octa Insight.
