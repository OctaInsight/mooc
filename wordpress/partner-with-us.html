Questions

Do learners pay anything?

No. Learners join, learn and receive their certificates for free. Only courses that are free for learners are published on the CloudEARTHi MOOC.

Can we use our own certificate design and logos?

Yes. Upload your design as an image or PDF, mark where the learner's name and details go, and every certificate is made on it.

Can we issue certificates for an event without a course?

Yes. Upload an Excel list of participants, and each person receives their own certificate by email.

Who runs the MOOC?

The CloudEARTHi MOOC is the MOOC of the CloudEARTHi initiative. It runs on Octa MOOC Studio, the tool for building courses and running the MOOC, which was designed and is paid for exclusively by Octa Insight, with no contribution from any project. Octa MOOC Studio is free for CloudEARTHi projects and offered at cost price to CloudEARTHi partners.

Who owns the course content?

You do. You keep the rights to your material; we publish it on the MOOC for as long as you wish to keep it there.

How are certificates checked?

Each certificate has a number and its own page at mooc.cloudearthi.com, which shows the learner, the course, your project as issuer, and that it was verified by the CloudEARTHi MOOC and Octa Insight.
