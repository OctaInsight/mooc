# CloudEARTHi MOOC, Octa MOOC Studio and other MOOCs: roles and funding

The agreed facts, in one place. The three web pages use them as follows:

- **Acknowledgement page** (mooc.cloudearthi.com): all of it, to show there is no conflict of interest.
- **Partner with us** (mooc.cloudearthi.com): who may publish and on what terms (sections 1, 2 and 4, short).
- **MOOC page on octainsight.com/mooc**: all three tools, with Octa's packages and prices.

## The three tools

1. **The CloudEARTHi MOOC** (mooc.cloudearthi.com): the MOOC of the CloudEARTHi initiative. It publishes only courses
   that are free for learners.
2. **Octa MOOC Studio**: the tool used to build the courses and to run the MOOC behind the scenes (courses, learners,
   certificates, badges and the database). It was designed by Octa Insight and is paid for exclusively by Octa
   Insight, with no contribution from any project.
3. **Other MOOCs built by Octa**: MOOCs that Octa Insight builds with Octa MOOC Studio for organisations and projects,
   under their own name, colours and web address. They have nothing to do with CloudEARTHi. The organisation that
   orders the MOOC pays for it and decides whether its courses are free or paid for learners; if paid, the money goes
   to that organisation, not to Octa.

## 1. Who may publish on the CloudEARTHi MOOC, and on what terms

| Who | Terms |
|---|---|
| CloudEARTHi projects | Free, always |
| CloudEARTHi partners | Free to publish their own free courses that are not linked to a CloudEARTHi project (courses from CloudEARTHi projects fall under the first row); a reduced price of €50 a year covers only database hosting and Octa MOOC Studio, below what they cost Octa (launch price 2027) |
| Other projects and organisations | May publish free courses; they pay for database hosting and technical help, either through one of Octa's packages (octainsight.com/mooc) or by having Octa Insight as a partner in their project |

Learners never pay on the CloudEARTHi MOOC.

## 2. Octa MOOC Studio

- Designed and paid for exclusively by Octa Insight; no project or EU grant has contributed to it.
- Free of charge for CloudEARTHi projects.
- At a reduced price for CloudEARTHi partners (€50 a year, below the real cost of database hosting and technical time).
- Other organisations pay for its use.

## 3. Other MOOCs built by Octa

- Ordered and paid for by the organisation; not part of CloudEARTHi and not funded by any CloudEARTHi project.
- The organisation decides whether learners pay. Payments go straight to the organisation, which as the seller
  charges and pays the VAT on course sales. Octa invoices only for building and running the MOOC.

## 4. EU funding and history (as on the Acknowledgment page)

- The CloudEARTHi MOOC student website was established in 2023–2024 with funding from BOOSTalent (project 10048,
