Questions

Do learners pay anything?

No. Learners join, learn and receive their certificates for free. Only the organisation publishing the course pays for its package.

What happens when our project ends?

With the EU project package, your courses and certificates stay online for 3 years after the end, already paid. After that you can continue with Hosting.

Can a project pay from its EU grant?

Often yes, as a service cost, if your grant agreement allows it. We send a quote and an invoice in the form your project needs.

Who owns the course content?

You do. You keep the rights to your material; we publish it on the MOOC for as long as your package runs.

How do the reduced rates work?

Say in your request which rate applies to you. CloudEARTHi partners and organisations in low- and lower-middle-income countries (World Bank list) get 75% off; non-profits for women, equality and inclusion in high-income countries get 50% off. Reduced rates cannot be combined.
