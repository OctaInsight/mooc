The technology behind Octa MOOC Studio Octa Proof of Learning: online learning that checks itself Learners move forward only when the system has confirmed that the learning took place. Read moreClose

Most online courses can be clicked through: play the video, scroll the PDF, move on. Octa MOOC Studio, designed and built by Octa Insight, works differently. It combines interactive video and documents, steps that cannot be skipped, answers checked on our side, automatic awards and verifiable certificates, all in one tool.

Questions inside the video

Teachers place questions at chosen moments in a YouTube video. The video pauses and waits for the answer. Skipping ahead brings the learner back to the question, and jumping forward does not count as watching.

Questions inside the documents

The same works for reading. Questions sit on specific pages of a PDF, so a document is read and understood, not just opened.

Progress the learner earns

Each lecture is a path of steps: watch, read, answer, hand in, join a live session. Key questions must be answered correctly before the next step opens. Answers are checked on our side, never in the learner's browser, so the right answers cannot be found in advance. Scores count first-try answers, a fair picture of what was understood.

A second chance, with a full record

Learners can retake a lecture to improve, within the limit the teacher sets. Every earlier attempt is kept, so teachers and organisations see the whole learning journey, not just the last try.

Certificates anyone can verify

Certificates and badges are awarded automatically when a learner meets the course's rules, such as the lectures finished and the minimum score. Each one has its own permanent verification page that employers, universities and funders can open to confirm it is genuine. Learners can share it on different social media channels, and the organisation can withdraw a certificate at any time.

A public learning profile

Each learner can have a public profile that shows all their certificates and badges in one place: a portfolio of verified learning to share with employers.

Your design, our verification

Courses and projects can use their own certificate design, with their funders' and partners' logos. The Studio places the learner's name, the course and the verification address on it, with no design software needed.

Certificates for events, from a list

For conferences, workshops and training days, organisers upload a participant list. Each person receives a verifiable certificate by email, with their role (speaker, participant, organiser), and a learner account is created for anyone who does not have one.

Evidence for your reports

Every learning step is recorded, so projects can report real figures: learners, completions, scores and certificates, rather than page views.

Questions

Do learners pay anything?

On your own MOOC, you decide whether courses are free or paid. On the CloudEARTHi MOOC, courses and certificates are always free for learners.

What is the difference between the two ways?

Your own MOOC has your name, colours and web address and only your courses, and you decide whether learners pay. On the CloudEARTHi MOOC, you publish free courses and pay only for database hosting and technical help.

If learners pay, who receives the money?

You do. We connect online payment to your own account, so payments go straight to you and Octa takes no share. As the seller, you charge and pay the VAT on course sales.

What happens when our project ends?

With the Project MOOC or the EU project package, your courses and certificates stay online for 3 years after the end, already paid. After that you can continue with Own MOOC or Hosting.

Can a project pay from its EU grant?

Often yes, as a service cost, if your grant agreement allows it. We send a quote and an invoice in the form your project needs. Even better: invite Octa into the consortium while you write the proposal.

Who owns the course content?

You do. You keep the rights to your material; we publish it on the MOOC for as long as your package runs.

How do the reduced rates work?

They apply to publishing on the CloudEARTHi MOOC. Say in your request which rate applies to you: organisations in low- and lower-middle-income countries (World Bank list) get 75% off; non-profits for women, equality and inclusion in high-income countries get 50% off. CloudEARTHi partners pay a reduced price of €50 a year. Reduced rates cannot be combined.
