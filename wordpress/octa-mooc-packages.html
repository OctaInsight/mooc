Questions

Do learners pay anything?

Not on the CloudEARTHi MOOC: learners join, learn and receive their certificates for free there. On your own MOOC, you decide whether courses are free or paid.

What is the difference between the two ways?

The CloudEARTHi MOOC is the MOOC of the CloudEARTHi initiative: it publishes only free courses, and you pay only for database hosting and technical help. Your own MOOC has your name, colours and web address and only your courses; it is separate from CloudEARTHi, and you decide whether learners pay.

Who pays for Octa MOOC Studio?

Octa Insight designed Octa MOOC Studio and pays for it alone, with no contribution from any project or grant. It is free for CloudEARTHi projects, offered at cost price to CloudEARTHi partners, and paid for by other organisations through the packages on this page.

If learners pay, who receives the money?

You do. We connect online payment to your own account, so payments go straight to you and Octa takes no share. As the seller, you charge and pay the VAT on course sales.

What happens when our project ends?

With the EU project package or the Project MOOC, your courses and certificates stay online for 3 years after the end, already paid. After that you can continue with Hosting or Own MOOC.

Can a project pay from its EU grant?

Often yes, as a service cost, if your grant agreement allows it. We send a quote and an invoice in the form your project needs. Even better: invite Octa into the consortium while you write the proposal.

Who owns the course content?

You do. You keep the rights to your material; we publish it on the MOOC for as long as your package runs.

How do the reduced rates work?

They apply to publishing on the CloudEARTHi MOOC. Say in your request which rate applies to you: organisations in low- and lower-middle-income countries (World Bank list) get 75% off; non-profits for women, equality and inclusion in high-income countries get 50% off. CloudEARTHi partners pay cost price. Reduced rates cannot be combined.
