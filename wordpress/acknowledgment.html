Acknowledgment

We thank the funders and partners whose support built the CloudEARTHi MOOC and its courses. This page explains how the MOOC is made up, who has funded each part over the years, and which projects funded the courses it hosts.

1. How the CloudEARTHi MOOC is built

The CloudEARTHi MOOC is made up of three parts:

CloudEARTHi MOOC

The student website where learners join courses, learn and receive certificates and badges. It runs on the Octa database and is managed with Octa MOOC Studio.

Octa MOOC Studio

The tool used to build the courses and run the MOOC: courses, learners, teachers, certificates and badges.

Octa database

The database that stores the courses, learners, progress and awards behind the MOOC.

The Octa database and Octa MOOC Studio are funded only and exclusively by Octa Insight AS, with no contribution from any EU project. The CloudEARTHi MOOC student website has a longer history, set out below.

2. History of the CloudEARTHi MOOC student website

  • 2023–2024The CloudEARTHi MOOC was established with funding from the BOOSTalent project (project number 10048, EIT HEI Initiative).
  • 2025The operating costs of the MOOC were covered by the Inspiring the Minds project (project number 2022-1-NO01-KA220-HED-000087789), funded by Erasmus+ under Key Action 2.
  • 2026The MOOC is fully covered by Octa Insight's own resources, with no contribution from any project.

3. Funding of the courses

The courses on the CloudEARTHi MOOC were developed in different projects, funded as follows:

  • BOOSTalent (project number 10048) was funded by the EIT HEI Initiative, under the administration and support of EIT RawMaterials.
  • Inspiring the Minds (project number 2022-1-NO01-KA220-HED-000087789) is funded by Erasmus+ under Key Action 2 (Cooperation among organisations and institutions). It is administered and supported by the Norwegian Directorate for Higher Education and Skills.
  • SEEDplus is funded by Horizon Europe (grant agreement ID 101100494) and falls under the administration of EISMEA, the European Innovation Council and SMEs Executive Agency.
  • Mind the Gap is funded by Horizon Europe (grant agreement ID 101215579) and falls under the administration of EISMEA, the European Innovation Council and SMEs Executive Agency.

Each course names the project it comes from on its course page.
