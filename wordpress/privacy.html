Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data the CloudEARTHi MOOC collects, why, who can see it, how long it is kept and what your rights are, under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Norwegian law.

1. Who is responsible

Octa Insight AS (organisation number 930 841 366, Strandvegen 53, 9007 Tromsø, Norway) is the data controller for the CloudEARTHi MOOC and for Octa accounts. Octa Insight develops and operates the platform for the CloudEARTHi initiative.

Questions about your data: sales@octainsight.com, or see Contact us.

2. What we collect

  • Your Octa account: name, email address and password. Your password is never stored in readable form.
  • Your learning: courses you join, your progress, quiz and assignment answers, and the certificates and badges you receive.
  • Data from course organisers: when an organisation issues certificates or badges for a course or event, it gives us the participants' names and email addresses and the award each person receives.
  • Earlier registrations: if you registered on the earlier version of the MOOC, the details you gave then, including any optional information for statistics, such as age group, gender, education or country.
  • Messages: what you write when you email us.
  • Technical data: when you visit, our web host records technical information such as your IP address, browser and the pages requested, to keep the site secure and working.

4. Who can see your data

  • Anyone with the link to a certificate or badge can see its verification page: your name, the course or event, the date and the issuer. Your profile page lists your awards in the same way.
  • Teachers and course administrators of a course you join can see your name, email, progress and results in that course.
  • The organisation that issued your certificate or badge can see that it was issued to you.
  • Octa Insight staff can access data when needed to run the platform and help you.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account and learning data: as long as your account exists. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete it within 30 days.
  • Certificates and badges: as long as the platform runs, so that their verification pages keep working, unless you ask us to delete them.
  • Your messages to us: up to two years after the matter is closed.
  • Technical logs: only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting.
  • Accounting records: as long as Norwegian bookkeeping law requires (currently five years).

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • get a copy of your personal data, and have it corrected;
  • have your data deleted, or its use restricted;
  • object to uses based on our legitimate interest;
  • receive the data you gave us in a machine-readable format;
  • withdraw any consent you have given, at any time.

Write to sales@octainsight.com. We answer within two weeks. You also have the right to complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet, or to the data protection authority in your own country.

8. Cookies and browser storage

  • The CloudEARTHi MOOC website sets only necessary cookies, for example to keep you signed in and to protect sign-in forms.
  • The course pages keep your sign-in in your browser's local storage, so you stay signed in between visits. Signing out removes it.
  • We use no analytics or advertising cookies. If we ever add them, we will ask for your consent first.

9. Security

All connections to the platform are encrypted (HTTPS). Access to the database is limited and goes only through controlled functions, and passwords are never stored in readable form. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will inform you and the authorities if a breach affects you, as the law requires.

10. Age

The platform is for people aged 16 and over.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version. We will tell you about important changes by email or a notice on the platform.
